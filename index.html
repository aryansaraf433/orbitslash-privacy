Privacy Policy for Orbit Slash

Last Updated: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Orbit Slash ("we", "our", or "the Game") handles your information when you download, install, and play our mobile application on Android devices.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

Orbit Slash does not require account registration with personal credentials. We do not collect or store personally identifiable information (PII) such as:

  • Your real name
  • Your email address or phone number
  • Your physical address or precise GPS location
  • Your contacts, photos, or personal files

2. Information Collected and Used

The Game may collect and process the following minimal non-personal information to enable core game mechanics:

  • Gameplay & Progression Data: High scores, currency, unlocked skins, and achievements are saved locally on your device storage (via local save files).
  • Public Leaderboard: If you choose to submit a high score, the game transmits your custom Pilot Alias (nickname) and score to our Supabase database to display public rankings. Please do not use your real name as your pilot alias.
  • Anonymous Technical Telemetry: Basic diagnostic telemetry (such as frame rate metrics and crash reports) may be gathered to troubleshoot device compatibility and optimize performance on low-end hardware.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertising

The Game integrates third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device in accordance with their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC): We display non-intrusive advertisements (banners, interstitials, and rewarded video ads). AdMob may collect and process device identifiers, Android Advertising ID (AD_ID), IP addresses, and diagnostic information to serve personalized or non-personalized ads.
    For more details on Google's privacy practices, visit: Google Privacy Policy and AdMob Policies.
  • Supabase: Used as a secure cloud database provider to store public high scores and leaderboards.

4. Permissions Used by the Application

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to fetch global leaderboard rankings and retrieve AdMob advertisements.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to verify active network connectivity before sending leaderboard scores or requesting ads.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID: Standard Android permission used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK for ad serving and fraud detection.

5. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Orbit Slash is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete the data.

6. Data Retention and Deletion Requests

All local progress data can be completely cleared at any time by clearing the game's app data or uninstalling the application from your Android device. If you wish to have a leaderboard score entry deleted from our public database, you can contact us with your pilot alias.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
