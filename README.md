# Privacy Policy for Orbit Slash
**Last Updated: October 11, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **Orbit Slash** ("we", "our", or "the Game") collects, uses, and safeguards information when you play our mobile application on Android devices.

---

### 1. Information We Do NOT Collect
Orbit Slash does not require user accounts with personal credentials. We do **not** collect or store personal data such as:
- Real name
- Email address or phone number
- Precise GPS location
- Device contacts, photos, or files

---

### 2. Information Collected and Used
The Game processes minimal non-personal information to support gameplay:
- **Local Progress:** Scores, credits, unlocked ships, and achievements are stored locally on your device.
- **Global Leaderboard:** Submitting a high score transmits your chosen Pilot Alias (nickname) and score to our Supabase database. Do not use your real name as your alias.
- **Anonymous Telemetry:** Non-personal technical performance metrics (e.g., FPS) to optimize gameplay on various devices.

---

### 3. Third-Party Services
The Game integrates the following third-party services:
- **Google AdMob (Google LLC):** Serves advertisements (banner, interstitial, rewarded). AdMob may collect and process device identifiers, advertising IDs (`AD_ID`), and IP addresses in accordance with [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Supabase:** Cloud database provider for hosting global leaderboards.

---

### 4. Permissions Used
- `INTERNET`: To fetch leaderboard data and load ads.
- `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`: To detect network availability.
- `AD_ID`: Used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK for ad serving and fraud prevention.

---

### 5. Children's Privacy
Orbit Slash does not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13.

---
